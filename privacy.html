Privacy Policy

Monthly OS · Last updated October 7, 2026

Monthly OS ("the app", "we") is operated by Mathys Lobeau, Namur, Belgium. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. Contact: mymonthlyos.app@gmail.com.

Data we handle

  • Account data. If you sign in with Apple, we receive a user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email (or Apple's private relay address). You can also use the app as a guest.
  • Content you enter. The financial information, goals, notes and settings you add in the app, so we can show and sync them.
  • Voice, camera and photos. Only if you use features that need the microphone, speech recognition, camera or photo library, and only after you grant permission in iOS. You can revoke it at any time in Settings.
  • Purchase data. Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription status. It receives an app user ID, purchase and subscription details, and basic device information. We never see your payment card.
  • Face ID. If you enable biometric lock, authentication happens on your device. We do not receive or store biometric data.

How we use it

  • To provide the app, sync your data and unlock Premium.
  • To generate AI insights from the content you enter. This content is sent to our AI provider only to produce your result.
  • To keep the app secure, fix bugs and respond to your requests.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Service providers

We rely on trusted processors to run the app: Apple (sign-in, payments), RevenueCat (subscription management), Supabase (data storage and authentication) and an AI provider (insights). They process data only on our instructions and under their own privacy terms. Data may be transferred outside your country, including to the United States, with appropriate safeguards where required.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by emailing us, and we will act on it within 30 days, except for records we must keep by law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EU/EEA and the UK under the GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Email us to exercise them. You may also complain to your data protection authority; in Belgium, that is the Autorité de protection des données (autoriteprotectiondonnees.be). California residents have similar rights under the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not sell or share personal data as those laws define it.

Children

Monthly OS is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 where local law requires), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

We use industry-standard measures such as encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
