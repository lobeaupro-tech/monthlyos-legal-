Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Premium subscription?

Open the app, go to the paywall or Settings, and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, select Monthly OS Premium and tap Cancel Subscription.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us from the address linked to your account and we will delete your data.
